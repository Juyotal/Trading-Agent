## ES (E-mini S&P 500 Futures)

### Instrument Characteristics
- Broadest US equity benchmark, more diversified than NQ
- Average daily range: 40-80 points
- More stable than NQ, less prone to extreme momentum
- Highly liquid — tightest spreads of any equity futures
- Most active during US regular trading hours (9:30 AM - 4:00 PM ET)

### Strategy Bias
- Mean-reversion works well on ES, especially around VWAP
- Trend days (strong directional moves all day) happen ~20% of sessions
- On range days, fading moves at Bollinger Band extremes is effective
- Morning reversal setups (9:45-10:15 AM ET) after initial move are common
- Respect overnight high/low as key reference levels

### Key Considerations
- Tighter stops than NQ (1-2x ATR is appropriate)
- VWAP is the single most important intraday level for ES
- Opening range (first 15-30 min) often defines the day's bias
- Major economic releases (NFP, FOMC, CPI) create binary events — prefer HOLD beforehand
- Sector rotation can create divergences with NQ — note if ES and NQ are moving together or apart
