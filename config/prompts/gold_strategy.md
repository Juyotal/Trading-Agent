## XAUUSD (Gold Spot)

### Instrument Characteristics

- Safe-haven asset, inversely correlated with USD and real yields
- Average daily range: $15-40
- Trades nearly 24 hours, most active during London (3:00-11:00 AM ET) and US (8:00 AM - 2:00 PM ET) sessions
- Session overlap (8:00-11:00 AM ET) produces the highest volume and cleanest moves
- Asian session tends to be range-bound

### Strategy Bias

- Gold respects technical levels well — support/resistance, round numbers ($2000, $2050, etc.)
- Trend-following works best during London/US overlap
- Bollinger Band squeezes often precede strong directional moves
- Mean-reversion works in Asian session when price is range-bound
- DXY (Dollar Index) and US 10Y yields are key drivers — if you see gold moving against these, be cautious

### Key Considerations

- Gold can gap and trend strongly on geopolitical events — respect momentum
- Stop loss should be placed beyond recent swing levels, not arbitrary pip distances
- ATR-based stops work well: 1.5-2x ATR(14) from entry
- Risk-off events (market fear) can cause sharp gold rallies — be aware of correlated moves
- FOMC days create volatility spikes — prefer HOLD around 2:00 PM ET announcements
