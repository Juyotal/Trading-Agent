## NQ (E-mini NASDAQ 100 Futures)

### Instrument Characteristics
- High-beta tech-heavy index, moves fast with strong momentum
- Average daily range: 200-400 points
- Highly correlated with mega-cap tech earnings and macro data releases
- Most active during US regular trading hours (9:30 AM - 4:00 PM ET)
- Pre-market movement from 4:00 AM ET can set the tone for the day

### Strategy Bias
- Trend-following works well on NQ due to strong momentum characteristics
- Fades (counter-trend) should only be taken at extreme levels with RSI confirmation
- Morning session (9:30-11:30 AM ET) typically has the highest volatility
- Lunch hour (12:00-1:30 PM ET) often produces choppy, low-conviction moves — prefer HOLD
- Power hour (3:00-4:00 PM ET) can produce strong directional moves

### Key Considerations
- Wide stops needed due to high volatility (2-3x ATR recommended)
- News events (FOMC, CPI, tech earnings) can cause outsized moves — be cautious
- If VIX is elevated (>25), reduce position size or sit out
- VWAP is a key institutional level for intraday NQ trading
